# Code of Conduct

These rules apply everywhere the project gathers: the issue trackers, pull requests, discussions and Discord.

* **Behave**: You are a guest in our community.
* **Patience**: Don't expect everyone to give you what you want immediately.
* **Thoughtful**: Try to answer your own questions before asking us.
* **On Topic**: Ask questions related to Red Eclipse only, we won't help you create a game.
* **Speak English**: One language unifies us, multiple languages create chaos.
* **No Slander**: Do NOT engage in blatant racism, political or religious debates, or engage in slander over the sex and/or orientation of anyone else.

## Reporting Problems

If someone breaks these rules, tell a moderator on [Discord](https://www.redeclipse.net/discord) or email contact@redeclipse.net. Don't argue it out in public. Maintainers may hide or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who keep breaking these rules.
