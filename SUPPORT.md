# Getting Support

The issue trackers are for **bug reports** and **feature requests**. Pick the matching form when you open an issue.

For anything else, these are the best places to ask:

* [Help and support discussions](https://github.com/redeclipse/base/discussions/categories/help-and-support), for installing, playing, mapping or running a server.
* [Ideas and feature requests discussions](https://github.com/redeclipse/base/discussions/categories/ideas-and-feature-requests), to talk an idea through before requesting it.
* [Discord](https://www.redeclipse.net/discord), to chat with players and developers live.

The [documentation](https://www.redeclipse.net/docs) and [FAQ](https://www.redeclipse.net/docs/FAQ) answer many common questions, and the [install guide](https://www.redeclipse.net/docs/Install-Guide) covers getting the game running.

Before you contribute, please read the [contributing guide](https://github.com/redeclipse/.github/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
