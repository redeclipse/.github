# Governance

Red Eclipse is a free, open source project made by volunteers. This document explains who looks after it and how decisions are made.

## Roles

* **Project lead**: Quinton Reeves ([qreeves](https://github.com/qreeves)) leads the project and has the final say when a decision can't be settled otherwise.
* **Core developers** have push access to every repository and administer the organisation, the official master server and the website. They review and merge pull requests and set the project's direction.
* **Developers** look after the data submodules and help triage issues.
* **Contributors** help triage issues, and maintain the community, promotional and bonus repositories.
* **Mappers** work on maps and the tools around them, and **world** members are the artists and writers working on the game's world and lore.
* **Moderators** keep the issue trackers, discussions, Discord and official servers in line with the [code of conduct](https://github.com/redeclipse/.github/blob/master/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and the [multiplayer guidelines](https://www.redeclipse.net/docs/gameplay/Multiplayer-Guidelines).

Anyone can take part by reporting issues, suggesting ideas, joining discussions or sending pull requests, as the [contributing guide](https://github.com/redeclipse/.github/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains.

## Decisions

* Most decisions happen in the open, in issues, pull requests and the [discussions](https://www.redeclipse.net/discuss). Anyone is welcome to give their view.
* Core developers decide whether a change is merged, based on how well it fits the game's [core principles](https://www.redeclipse.net/docs/Core-Principles), its quality and the work it would take to maintain.
* Larger changes to gameplay may be put to the community as a poll first. Issues waiting on a poll's result are labelled `status: poll implementation`.
* When core developers can't agree, the project lead decides.

## Joining a Team

People who contribute well and regularly in an area may be invited to join the matching team. There's no application process: keep contributing, and talk to a core developer on [Discord](https://www.redeclipse.net/discord) if you'd like to take on more.

## Changing These Documents

The contributing guide, code of conduct, this document and the other community files apply to every repository in the organisation. Changes to them are proposed as pull requests to [redeclipse/.github](https://github.com/redeclipse/.github) and decided by the core developers.
